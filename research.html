Research

Research directions

Developing optical technologies for structural, vascular, molecular, and multimodal biomedical imaging.

OCT

Optical Coherence Tomography

High-resolution structural and functional tissue imaging for dermatology, oncology, and vascular disease, including OCT angiography and quantitative microvascular analysis.

PA

Photoacoustic Imaging

Optical absorption contrast for visualizing molecular composition, vasculature, and tumor biology across cells, organoids, animal models, and patients.

MI

Multimodal Imaging

Integrated imaging systems that combine complementary structural, functional, and molecular contrasts, including optical coherence photoacoustic microscopy.
