About

Biomedical imaging researcher, project coordinator, educator, and academic community leader.

Biography

I am an Associate Professor at the Medical University of Vienna. My research combines imaging physics, instrumentation, artificial intelligence, and clinical translation, with a particular focus on optical coherence tomography (OCT), OCT angiography, and photoacoustic imaging. I have coordinated international collaborations and supervised master’s and doctoral researchers.
